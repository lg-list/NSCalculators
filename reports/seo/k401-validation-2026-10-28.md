## Changes

- Separate pure projection and withdrawal calculations from DOM input reading. Required blanks, unsupported values and fractional projection/year-end match ages produce a corrective message rather than substituted values. Explicit zero balances, contributions and withdrawals remain valid.
- Preserve monthly contribution timing and the default projected balance of $1,504,757.56, monthly retirement income of $6,768.45 and employer deposits of $118,131.21. Use stable equivalent-rate and growing-annuity calculations near zero rates.
- Display signed investment losses. Show a signed net and additional funds required when entered combined taxes exceed the gross distribution, instead of hiding that shortfall behind zero cash.
- Keep the 59.5 withdrawal boundary and clarify the verified calendar-year separation assumption. A person aged 54.5 can be in the year they turn 55; the form cannot verify birth date, separation year or employer-plan eligibility. Other exceptions assume the full entered amount qualifies. Partial exemptions require separate modeling. No eligibility certification is implied.
- Clear now removes stale summary cards, schedule and chart. Employer-match limits and existing numerical outputs remain covered by their earlier regression suite; pay-period selection is validated.
- Fold secondary projection and exception assumptions; retain 16px mobile inputs and the existing theme. Remove the redundant category badge on this page. All three modes have Calculate visible within a 844px-high viewport at desktop, 390px and 320px widths, with no horizontal overflow.
- Add original timing, zero/blank, withdrawal examples and scope explanations to the existing page. No new indexable URL or title rewrite.

## Model boundaries and primary sources

The projection remains an **uncapped salary-based scenario**, not a contribution-compliance forecast: it does not predict future IRS limits. The separate employer-match mode models stated 2026 limits. No statutory limit was updated in this repair. Salary-growth, investment, inflation and retirement-return inputs are bounded to -99% through 100%; whole projection ages and documented numeric bounds are explicit.

Withdrawal uses fully taxable gross distributions and entered marginal-rate approximations, not brackets, withholding or a tax return. [IRS exceptions guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-exceptions-to-tax-on-early-distributions), freshly checked October 28, explains age 59½, separation in or after the calendar year of 55, qualified-plan versus IRA treatment, disability and exceptions with limited qualifying amounts. Special public-safety rules are not inferred. [IRS contribution limits](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-401k-and-profit-sharing-plan-contribution-limits) and [high-compensation matching guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/401k-plans-deferrals-and-matching-when-compensation-exceeds-the-annual-limit) inform existing scope; the latter page's older dollar examples must not be interpreted as 2026 values.
