## Primary task references

[Vulcan Materials](https://www.vulcanmaterials.com/construction-materials/product-calculators) provides aggregate quantity estimation by project dimensions and states that local project conditions and supplier review affect estimates. This establishes task relevance, not a density value or a universal material recommendation.

[Penn State's DGLVR calculator](https://dirtandgravel.psu.edu/general-resources/dglvr-materials-calculator/) separates loose and compacted input basis and gives loose cubic yards/US tons. It allows custom density/compaction inputs and explains that its listed factors are regional averages varying by supplier. This supports requiring clearly defined material state and supplier-specific conversion rather than treating cubic yards as a fixed number of tons. Do not transplant its PA average factors nationwide.

The [Home Depot mulch calculator](https://www.homedepot.com/calculator/mulch) search result exposes dimensions and square-footage modes, but direct fetch was 403; its complete interactive behavior was not verified and no copied copy/design is proposed. The independent volume example is geometry: 200 ft² × 3/12 ft = 50 ft³ = 50/27 yd³; a user-entered 2 ft³ bag requires 25 bags before any allowance. This is not a horticultural depth recommendation.
