# NS Calculators SEO Audit

Date: 2026-09-17
Primary market: United States
Canonical domain: https://nscalculators.com/

## Executive summary

The site is technically crawlable, but its original indexation strategy exposed more than 5,000 templated pages to a new domain. Only 174 unique keywords in the supplied Google Keyword Planner export have positive search volume. The revised build keeps every calculator available to users while limiting the XML sitemap to the strongest 400 calculator pages and applying `noindex,follow` to low-demand variants.

## Keyword findings

- 174 unique keyword-to-page mappings
- 9,600 combined average monthly searches in the supplied positive-keyword export
- Highest-volume terms: `feet to meters calculator` (500) and `compound interest calculator` (500)
- Conversion calculators: 124 keywords / 6,650 monthly searches
- Time and date calculators: 16 keywords / 800 monthly searches
- Financial calculators: 4 keywords / 650 monthly searches
- Automotive calculators: 12 keywords / 600 monthly searches
- Construction calculators: 9 keywords / 450 monthly searches
- Electrical calculators: 6 keywords / 300 monthly searches

The complete mapping is stored in `exports/keyword-page-map.json`.

## Data caveat

The supplied forecast CSV is configured for China, not the United States. The current mapping is useful for prioritization, but a new Keyword Planner export should be generated with United States location, English language, and Google Search network before making final traffic forecasts.

## Implemented technical changes

- Reduced the XML sitemap from 5,038 URLs to 415 unique canonical URLs.
- Replaced the sitemap index with one standard `urlset` file.
- Removed misleading generated `lastmod` dates.
- Added `noindex,follow` to 4,623 low-demand or utility pages.
- Confirmed zero sitemap/noindex conflicts.
- Confirmed zero duplicate titles or descriptions among sitemap URLs.
- Confirmed zero broken internal links after regeneration.
- Confirmed one H1 and a self-referencing canonical on every sitemap page.
- Removed obsolete `meta keywords` tags.
- Added Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, and SoftwareApplication relationships.
- Unified the public brand name as NS Calculators.
- Reduced homepage HTML from about 1.25 MB to about 16 KB by moving search data to a lazy-loaded JSON asset.
- Added data-driven common-value tables to unit conversion pages.

## Priority content plan

1. Expand `feet-to-meters-calculator` with reverse conversion, feet-and-inches input, copyable results, and authoritative unit references.
2. Expand `compound-interest-calculator` with compounding frequency, contribution timing, annual schedule, and scenario comparison.
3. Strengthen automotive pages with visible source and model-year assumptions.
4. Add authoritative references to financial, health, electrical, and construction pages.
5. Re-run US keyword research and use Search Console query data to promote additional pages only after demand is verified.
